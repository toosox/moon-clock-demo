# Moon Clock Demo 0.1.0-demo1

Free demonstration builds for Linux and Windows.

## Included

- live local date and time;
- Moon graphic and current phase;
- waxing or waning direction;
- next major phase with local date and time; and
- calendar date preview.

Lunar age and illumination percentage are available in the paid edition and
are not displayed by these demo builds.

## Release files

- `moon-clock-demo_0.1.0~demo1_all.deb`
- `MoonClock-Demo-Setup-0.1.0-demo1.exe`
